How It All Began

It started with a coffee.

Elena and Adrian met over iced coffee on an ordinary afternoon in Manila. What started as one coffee turned into late-night conversations, spontaneous weekend trips, missed trains, long drives, and eventually a collection of places that became part of their story.

Seven years later, they've collected more memories than souvenirs—and they're ready for their next adventure.

01 2019 • MANILA, PHILIPPINES

First Chapter

MANILA

"Where it all started"

Their first weekend together wasn't exactly planned. They got lost looking for a little café, argued over directions, laughed about it five minutes later, and somehow ended the day already planning their next trip.

Still can't agree on who got us lost.
02 2021 • KYOTO, JAPAN

Finding Rhythm

KYOTO

"We learned to slow down"

No packed itinerary. No rushing from one place to another. Just bicycles, quiet streets, too much matcha, and an entire afternoon spent getting nowhere in particular.

Our favorite day had absolutely no plans.
03 2022 • BALI, INDONESIA

The Unplanned

BALI

"The trip we almost didn't take"

A last-minute decision, a very questionable hotel booking, and one of the best weeks they've ever had together. They learned that the best memories aren't always the ones you plan.

10/10 would book the questionable hotel again.
04 2024 • PARIS, FRANCE

The Realization

PARIS

"Somewhere between here and there"

They came for the city. They stayed out too late, ate dessert before dinner, walked until their feet hurt, and watched the sunset together. Somewhere along the way, Adrian realized he didn't really care where they were—as long as Elena was beside him.

05 2025 • NEW YORK, USA

Laughter in Chaos

NEW YORK

"Our kind of chaos"

Their first trip where everything seemed to go wrong. Delayed flights. Wrong subway. Rain without an umbrella. And somehow, they laughed through all of it.

Definitely not our smoothest trip. Definitely one of our favorites.
