Chapter 01 — Footsteps & Memories
Footsteps
Moments collected along the way
Manila, Philippines • 2020
It Started With Coffee

We met over iced coffee on an ordinary afternoon in Manila. Neither of us knew that one simple coffee would turn into countless conversations, spontaneous afternoon drives, inside jokes, and memories we would hold close forever.

Quiet Observation: We talked for so long that the café closed around us without us noticing the passage of time.
Where afternoon turned to evening
El Nido, Palawan • 2022
Unplanned Rainstorms

From quiet kayak trips through hidden lagoons to getting caught in a sudden tropical downpour on a boat ride back. We laughed through the storm and learned how serene life feels when you share the unexpected with the right person.

Quiet Observation: Watching the rain fall over the open sea with fresh coconut water in hand.
Rain over quiet waters
Kyoto, Japan • 2024
A Wrong Exit & A Promise

We took a wrong train exit and found ourselves in a quiet hillside garden surrounded by autumn leaves. Away from the crowd, under the golden light, Adrian asked the question that set our future in motion.

Quiet Observation: A gentle breeze, warm matcha dango, and a quiet "Yes."
The afternoon light remembered
Manila • January 31, 2028
